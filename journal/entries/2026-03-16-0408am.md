- Green dye mixing has progressed from loaner mixer failure to new mixer procurement to first full batch in one week — structured DOE tracking of RPM, duration, and temperature would accelerate convergence vs. current trial-and-error
- The JobNotes schema upgrade (spec prefix + tolerances + reference formulas) positions the database for automated pre-run analysis — comparing incoming specs against historical successes with similar geometry is a high-leverage tool for reducing first-run failures
- A-suffix rerun tracking materially improved visibility on original jobs, same-job reruns, and missing rate/yield follow-up
- Wire and die condition, plus mechanical stability, were more predictive of tight-wall outcomes this week than cure changes alone
- The measurement change now has a defined direction, but tooling coverage and customer report alignment still need to be closed before rollout is stable
