# EMS-SW007 Polyamic Resin Solution Handling Rev A.pdf

### Summary of Polyamic Resin Solution Handling (SW007 Rev A)

This document outlines procedures for handling polyamic acid resin solution (also known as polyimide resin solution) and its associated wastes at the Chattanooga facility.

**Key Operational Points for Training:**

🧪 **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)**
- Required: Butyl or laminated PE/EVOH gloves, safety glasses or face shield.
- Recommended: Chemical-resistant apron.

🛢️ **Waste Containers**
- Use a 55-gallon closed-top metal drum (1A1, e.g., Uline S-10759RT or equivalent).
- Drum must be on a spill containment pallet (e.g., Uline H-4865 or equivalent).

🏷️ **Labeling**
- Drum must be labeled with:
    - "Non-Regulated Waste"
    - "Waste Polyamic Resin Solution"
- Labels can be preprinted (Figure 1, e.g., Grainger 8EHM1) or legibly handwritten.
- Enter "Zeus" as the shipper and the facility address.
- This material is **not DOT Regulated for shipment**.

🗑️ **Handling Waste at Point of Generation**
- Residual solution from raw material containers or the process is added to the 55-gallon drum.
- Containers can be drained through an open funnel as it's non-hazardous waste.
- Ensure bungs have a gasket.
- **Drum is full** when ~3 inches of headspace remain.
- Torque bungs to **20 ft-lbs**.

🗓️ **Waste Storage and Pickup**
- Move full drums to the waste storage area within **3 calendar days** of becoming full.
- Full drums must be placed on secondary containment in the storage area.
- Schedule pickup of accumulated waste drums within **two months**.

✍️ **Record Keeping**
- Maintain a **logbook** of daily waste weight or volume.
- Record the weight of full drums for the **Bill-Of-Lading (BOL)** to ensure accurate tracking.
- All waste determinations, manifests, and training records must be retained until facility closure.

**Note:** The waste generated from this process is classified as "Non-Regulated" under RCRA and has no hazardous characteristics or listed waste codes, specific to the procedures outlined in this document for the Chattanooga facility.
